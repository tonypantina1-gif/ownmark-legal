Ownmark Privacy Policy

Effective 2 November 2026

In short: Ownmark does not collect, store or share any of your personal data. Everything you add stays on your iPhone. There is no account, no server, and no analytics, advertising or tracking code in the app.

Who we are

Ownmark is an iOS app for keeping track of the things you own, their warranties, receipts and maintenance. In this policy, "we" and "us" means the developer of Ownmark. You can reach us at aafdigital@proton.me.

Information you add to the app

You can add details about items you own, such as names, brands, model and serial numbers, purchase dates, prices, retailers, the credit card type you paid with (never the card number), rooms, maintenance reminders, warranty claims and notes. You can also add photos of serial plates and receipts, PDF receipts and owner's manuals.

All of this is stored only on your device, inside the app's private storage, protected by Apple's file encryption. We never receive it and cannot see it.

Camera, photos and files

  • Camera: used only when you choose to scan a serial plate or receipt. Images are saved on your device.
  • Photos: Ownmark uses Apple's photo picker, so it only receives the specific photos you choose. It cannot see the rest of your library.
  • Files: when you import a PDF or image, Ownmark keeps a copy in its private storage on your device.

On-device processing

Text in your photos is read on your iPhone using Apple's Vision framework. On supported devices, Ownmark may ask Apple's on-device language model to suggest a product name and category. This processing happens entirely on your iPhone; nothing is sent to us or to any other server for this purpose.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Ownmark schedules reminders (for example, a warranty about to expire, a filter change, or the end of a free trial) directly on your device. No notification data passes through a server.

Purchases

Subscriptions and one-time purchases of Ownmark Pro are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, payment details or Apple Account information. The app only checks with Apple whether Pro is active on your account. Apple's handling of your purchase is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Data we collect

None. Ownmark has no account system and does not transmit your data. It contains no third-party SDKs, analytics, advertising or tracking. We do not sell or share data, because we have none to sell or share.

If you have chosen in iOS Settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may provide us with anonymous, aggregated crash reports and usage statistics. These do not identify you and do not include anything you've stored in Ownmark.

Backups

Like other apps, Ownmark's data can be included in your device backups (for example iCloud Backup or a backup to your computer) if you have backups turned on. Those backups are managed by Apple or by you, not by us.

Keeping or deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete individual items, receipts or claims inside the app, or delete everything by removing the app from your iPhone. Because we hold no copy, we cannot restore data you delete.

Your rights

Under laws such as the UK GDPR, the EU GDPR and the California Consumer Privacy Act, you have rights to access, correct and delete your personal data. Because Ownmark keeps your data only on your device, you can exercise these rights directly in the app at any time. If you have any questions, contact us at aafdigital@proton.me.

Children

Ownmark is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Ownmark changes how data is handled, we will update this page and the effective date above before that version is released. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.

Contact

Questions about privacy: aafdigital@proton.me
