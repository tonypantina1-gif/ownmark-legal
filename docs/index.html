Common questions

Where is my data stored?
Only on your iPhone. Ownmark has no account and no server, so we can't see or recover your data. Keep your device backups turned on if you want a copy.

How do I restore Ownmark Pro on a new device?
Sign in with the same Apple Account, open the Pro screen and tap Restore.

How do I cancel my subscription?
Open iOS Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Ownmark.

Can my family use Pro?
Yes. Ownmark Pro supports Family Sharing for up to six people in your Apple family.

The scanner misread a number. What should I do?
Tap the field on the review screen to correct it. Fields marked "Check this" are the ones Ownmark was least sure about.
